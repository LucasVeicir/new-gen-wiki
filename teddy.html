Teddy Knight feels calm, distant, and almost emotionless, as if nothing bothers him. He carries a quiet confidence that borders on arrogance, assuming he’s above players without needing to prove it. He rarely shows strong emotion, but when challenged, he becomes sharply focused and slightly intimidating, switching from detached to precise and dangerous in an instant. His mentality is rooted in absolute selflessness and anti-egoism, making him the exact ideological opposite of Blue Lock's strikers. Rejecting personal glory, he views himself as a "worthless trash doll" or a tool whose sole purpose is to be used by his teammates to secure victory for the collective. He views self-absorbed "egoists" as destructive forces chasing empty validation, confidently asserting that his detached, collective-focused system of football is fundamentally superior.

His obsession with money stems from his upbringing; he was born into a miserable household and, after being abandoned by his parents, was forced to live in the streets. He spent his time swindling people out of their money for survival, but eventually, he lost hope in life and was ready to die. Fortunately, he was found by Snuffy, who offered him help in exchange for playing football with him, with Snuffy paying for his signature golden teeth. Ever since, he has gained immense respect for Snuffy, not hesitating to defend him after Baro called him a coward and rejected his proposal to succeed him. After Snuffy declares his resignation of retirement, Lorenzo offers Baro a gold tooth for the gratitude of seeing him happy.
